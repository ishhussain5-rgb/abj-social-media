Bespoke media walls in Peterborough

ABJ Kitchens & Bedrooms designs, makes and fits bespoke media walls for living rooms across Peterborough, Stamford, Market Deeping, Huntingdon and the surrounding villages. Every wall is built around your screen, with storage for consoles and speakers, LED lighting and an electric fire where suitable.

Because we make the joinery in our own workshop, we can match wall panelling and storage elsewhere in the room. Our own team fits every media wall, backed by our 12-month guarantee.

How much does a bespoke media wall cost?

Cost depends on wall size, storage, finish, lighting, fireplace and technical requirements. We provide a clear quote after measuring your room and understanding the specification.

Can you include an electric fireplace?

Yes, where suitable. We can design the joinery around an electric fireplace and consider clearances, access and ventilation. Appliance instructions must be followed.

Can you hide all the TV cables?

Yes, cable management is planned into the design. We consider routes for power, HDMI, network cables, consoles, routers and other devices.

Do you help with TV mounting?

We can design the media wall around your screen size and mounting requirements. If specialist electrical or AV work is required, it must be completed safely by a qualified person.

Can you add LED lighting?

Yes. LED lighting can be included in display shelving, niches or around feature areas where suitable.

How long does installation usually take?

Most installations take a few days, depending on size and specification. Larger media walls with lighting, storage and fireplace details may take longer.
