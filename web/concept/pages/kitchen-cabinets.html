In detail

Kitchen cabinets in Peterborough

We make bespoke kitchen cabinets and kitchen units for homes, landlords and builders across Peterborough and the surrounding area. Every cabinet is made to measure in our own workshop from 18mm board, assembled and rigid, with soft-close hinges and drawers. Doors come in matt, gloss, Shaker, handleless and wood-effect finishes, and the inside can be matched to the door colour.

Choose full supply and fit, or supply only if you already have a fitter. If you are replacing a whole kitchen, start with our kitchen design service and see the kitchens page for styles and a guide price.

Are your kitchen cabinets flat-pack?

No. Every cabinet is made from 18mm board and assembled in our Peterborough workshop, so it arrives rigid and ready to fit.

Can you make cabinets in non-standard sizes?

Yes. Every cabinet is made to the width, height and depth your room needs, including extra-high wall units and tall units up to the ceiling.

Can I buy cabinets without fitting?

Yes. We supply cabinets, doors and panels on their own for your own fitter or builder. Delivery is quoted separately.

Can you replace just some of the cabinets in my kitchen?

Often, yes. Send us photos and measurements on WhatsApp and we will tell you whether we can match the doors and finish.

What hinges and drawer runners do you use?

Soft-close hinges and runners as standard, from brands such as Blum, GTV and Häfele depending on the design.

How much do bespoke kitchen cabinets cost?

It depends on how many cabinets you need, their sizes and the door finish. You receive an itemised quote that prices each cabinet after we measure up.

Which areas do you cover?

We are based in Peterborough and work across an 80-mile radius, including Stamford, Oundle, Market Deeping, Bourne, Spalding, Huntingdon, St Ives, Ely, Wisbech, Oakham and Cambridge.
