Walk-in wardrobes and dressing rooms in Peterborough

ABJ Kitchens & Bedrooms designs, makes and fits walk-in wardrobes and dressing rooms for homes across Peterborough, Stamford, Oundle, Market Deeping, Huntingdon and the surrounding villages. A walk-in wardrobe can fill a spare room or box room, part of a large bedroom, or a loft, and every unit is made to measure in our own Peterborough workshop.

We plan open hanging, drawers, shoe walls, dressing tables, islands, mirrors and lighting around what you own. Pair your dressing room with sliding wardrobes in the bedroom or a complete fitted bedroom.

How much space do I need for a walk-in wardrobe?

Less than most people think. A walk-in can work along one wall of a box room, in an L-shape or U-shape in a larger room, or in a loft. We will tell you what fits at the free design visit.

Can you turn a box room or spare bedroom into a dressing room?

Yes. Box rooms and spare bedrooms make excellent dressing rooms. We plan hanging, drawers, shoe storage and a dressing table around the window and door.

Can you add lighting?

Yes. We build LED lighting into shelves, hanging rails and mirrors, and can wire it to switch on when you open a door or enter the room.

Do walk-in wardrobes have doors?

They can be fully open, have doors on some sections, or sit behind a sliding or hinged door to the room. We will show you the options in 3D.

How much does a walk-in wardrobe cost?

The price depends on the size of the room, the finishes and how much is drawers, shelving or hanging. After the free design visit you receive an itemised quote. Interest-free finance is available on projects over £6,000 with a 50% deposit, subject to status.

Which areas do you cover?

We are based in Peterborough and fit across an 80-mile radius, including Stamford, Oundle, Market Deeping, Bourne, Spalding, Huntingdon, St Ives, Ely, Wisbech, Oakham and Cambridge.
