Planning a kitchen or bedroom in King’s Lynn

Older houses in the centre of King’s Lynn often have high ceilings, deep alcoves and walls that are not quite straight. We measure every alcove and wall, build tall pieces to the full ceiling height and scribe them to the walls on site.

In Gaywood, South Wootton and North Wootton, many homes have a kitchen that is ready for an update and bedrooms that would benefit from fitted storage. We plan around the existing plumbing and sockets where we can, which keeps the cost down.

Do you cover King’s Lynn and the villages around it?

Yes. King’s Lynn is about 36 miles from our workshop, around 50 minutes along the A47, and we cover Gaywood, South Wootton, North Wootton, West Lynn and Terrington St Clement.

Can you fit furniture in an older house with high ceilings?

Yes. We build tall wardrobes and alcove units to the full ceiling height, in sections if the stairs are narrow, and scribe them to the walls so there are no gaps.

How long does a kitchen take from design to fitting?

Once you approve the design, the kitchen takes four to six weeks to make, and fitting usually takes one to two weeks.

How much does a fitted kitchen cost?

A compact bespoke kitchen, made and fitted, starts from around £2,500, and a typical medium kitchen with quartz worktops comes to around £7,000 to £9,500. You get an itemised quote after the free design visit.

Can I see your work before I decide?

Yes. Our showroom is at 3 Brassey Close, Peterborough PE1 2AZ, about 50 minutes from King’s Lynn. We’re open Monday to Friday 8am to 6pm and Saturday 10am to 2pm. Call 01733 686767 before you travel.
