How much does a media wall cost in Peterborough?

A compact TV unit with shelves, made and fitted, starts from around £1,000. Units either side of a chimney breast usually come to around £1,900 to £2,800, a floor-to-ceiling wall around £2,500 to £3,500, and a wall-to-wall media wall around £3,200 to £4,500. Lighting, glass doors, slatted panels and the electric fire are extra.

What is included in your media wall prices?

The cabinets and panels made to measure in our workshop, the finish you choose, and delivery and fitting by our own team. Lighting, glass doors, slatted panels, drawers and the electric fire are priced as extras, and each one is shown on your quote.

How much does it cost to add an electric fire?

The fire itself depends on the make and size you choose. We build it into the joinery and price it with your design, so you can see the full cost before you decide.

Do painted or gloss finishes cost more?

Yes, a little. Woodgrain and matt finishes cost least; painted-look doors add around 10% and high gloss around 20%.

Can you hide the TV cables?

Yes. We plan the cable routes at the design visit so the wires run inside the media wall rather than across it.

Is finance available on a media wall?

Yes. Interest-free finance for 12 months is available through Ideal4Finance on projects over £6,000 with a 50% deposit, subject to status.

Which areas do you cover?

We are based in Peterborough and work across an 80-mile radius, including Stamford, Oundle, Market Deeping, Bourne, Spalding, Huntingdon, St Ives, Ely, Wisbech, Oakham and Cambridge.
