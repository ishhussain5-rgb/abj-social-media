Shaker or in-frame?

Two framed looks, made to measure

On a Shaker kitchen the doors sit on the front of the cabinets. On an in-frame kitchen each cabinet has a solid face frame and the doors close inside it, which gives a more traditional, furniture-like look and usually costs more because of the extra joinery. We can show you both at the design visit or in our showroom.

What is a Shaker kitchen?

A kitchen with Shaker doors: a simple square frame around a flat, recessed centre panel. It is one of the most popular kitchen styles because it suits both older and newer homes.

What is the difference between Shaker and in-frame?

On a Shaker kitchen the doors sit on the front of the cabinets. On an in-frame kitchen the doors close inside a solid frame around each cabinet, which looks more traditional and usually costs more because of the extra joinery.

Can you paint a Shaker kitchen any colour?

Yes. You choose the colour at the design visit, and the inside of the cabinets can be matched to the doors if you like.

What handles go with a Shaker kitchen?

Cup handles, knobs and bar handles all work. Brass, black and chrome are the most popular finishes, and we bring samples to the design visit.

How much does a Shaker kitchen cost?

A compact bespoke kitchen, made and fitted, starts from around £2,500, and a typical medium kitchen with quartz worktops comes to around £7,000 to £9,500. Your exact price comes with your 3D design.
