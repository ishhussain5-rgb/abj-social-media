Furniture built for the way Oakham homes are actually built.

Rutland has very little standard housing. One street gives you an ironstone cottage with thick walls and a ceiling that drops under the eaves; the next gives you a recent build off Barleythorpe Road with a builder-standard kitchen crying out for proper drawers. Local property types

Six kinds of Oakham home, six very different briefs

The right answer for a Grade II cottage near the Market Place is not the right answer for a new build in Barleythorpe. Here is how we approach the homes we see most often.

Period

Ironstone & limestone cottages

Uneven walls, low beams and deep reveals. We survey to the millimetre, scribe panels to the plaster and keep cabinet heights sympathetic to the ceiling, so nothing looks forced into a room that predates it by centuries.

Town centre

Terraces & townhouses

Narrow galley kitchens near the town centre benefit from full-height storage, slimline appliance housings and pale, light-reflecting fronts. We plan delivery and access around tight lanes and limited parking.

New build

Barleythorpe & modern estates

Developer kitchens are rarely short of cupboards and always short of drawers. We replace or extend them with deep pan drawers, island seating and wardrobes that use the full ceiling height.

Rural

Barn conversions & farmhouses

Large open-plan rooms need furniture with presence. Islands, tall banks, boot rooms and utility runs are designed to hold their own against exposed timber and generous floor areas.

Lettings

Rutland Water holiday lets

Fast turnarounds need hard-wearing surfaces, robust hinges and finishes that survive constant use. We specify for durability and install to a fixed programme between bookings.

Family

Homes near Oakham School

Sports kit, instruments, uniforms and homework all need somewhere to live. A boot room, utility storage and a proper study corner usually do more for daily life than another wall unit.

Do you cover Oakham and the rest of Rutland?

Yes. Oakham is roughly 25 miles from our Peterborough workshop, about 40 minutes via the A47 and A606, well inside our 80-mile installation radius. We cover the whole of LE15, including Uppingham, Langham, Barleythorpe, Cottesmore, Empingham, Whissendine, Exton and the villages around Rutland Water.

Is the design survey in Oakham really free?

Yes, and there is no obligation. A designer visits your home, measures the room and discusses storage, style, appliances and budget. Most projects include 3D visuals so you can see the layout before anything is made, and no deposit is taken at that stage.

Can you work with period stone cottages and listed properties?

Yes. Made-to-measure furniture suits older Rutland homes, where walls lean, floors fall away and ceilings drop under the eaves. We scribe end panels to the wall, adjust plinths to uneven floors and design around beams and deep reveals. If your property is listed or sits in a conservation area, consents go through Rutland County Council and we work to what your consent allows.

How much does a fitted kitchen cost in Oakham?

As a guide, a straightforward fitted kitchen starts from around £5,000, while larger kitchens with premium worktops and integrated appliances cost more. Wardrobes start from around £1,400 and media walls from around £1,000. You receive an itemised quotation after your survey.

How long will my project take?

Most projects are made in four to six weeks from approved design, with fitting then taking one to two weeks, because we manufacture in-house rather than ordering from a third party. Installation takes a few days for wardrobes or a media wall, and longer for a full kitchen involving plumbing, electrics and worktop templating.

Can I visit a showroom before I commit?

Yes. Our showroom and workshop are at 3 Brassey Close, Peterborough, PE1 2AZ, around 40 minutes from Oakham, where you can see finishes, hardware and construction in person. Please call 01733 686767 ahead so a designer is free when you arrive.

Do you take on holiday lets and rental properties around Rutland Water?

Yes. We work with landlords and holiday-let owners on durable, repeatable furniture packages, and run a separate HMO renovation service. Installation can be scheduled around bookings or void periods to keep lost nights to a minimum.

What guarantee comes with the furniture?

Furniture we manufacture carries our 12-month guarantee covering defects in materials and workmanship. Doors, cabinets, appliances, worktops and other components can carry longer manufacturer warranties, depending on what you choose, all confirmed in writing with your quotation.
