Local know-how

Planning a kitchen or bedroom in Fenland

Many homes in the old centres of March, Whittlesey and Chatteris are Victorian terraces and cottages, with narrow kitchens, chimney breasts and alcoves. We build larger pieces in sections so they get up older stairs, and scribe panels to the walls so there are no gaps.

On the newer estates on the edges of each town, the starting point is usually a basic builder’s kitchen and bedrooms with no built-in storage. A new kitchen planned around the existing plumbing, fitted wardrobes or a media wall make the biggest difference there.

Do you cover March, Whittlesey and Chatteris?

Yes. Whittlesey is about 6 miles from our workshop, March about 15 and Chatteris about 25, and we cover the villages around them, including Coates, Eastrea, Wimblington, Doddington and Manea.

Can you fit furniture in an older terrace with uneven walls?

Yes. We measure carefully, build larger runs in sections so they fit up narrow stairs, and scribe panels to the walls on site so there are no gaps.

Can you replace the kitchen in a newer home?

Yes. We plan the new kitchen around the existing plumbing and sockets where we can, which keeps the cost down, and we can measure for wardrobes or a media wall at the same visit.

How much does a fitted kitchen cost?

A compact bespoke kitchen, made and fitted, starts from around £2,500, and a typical medium kitchen with quartz worktops comes to around £7,000 to £9,500. You get an itemised quote after the free design visit.

Can I visit your showroom?

Yes. Our showroom is at 3 Brassey Close, Peterborough PE1 2AZ, about 15 minutes from Whittlesey. We’re open Monday to Friday 8am to 6pm and Saturday 10am to 2pm. Call 01733 686767 before you travel.
