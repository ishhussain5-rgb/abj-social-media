What is the best under-stairs storage?

For most staircases, pull-out drawers or tall pull-out towers are the best use of the space, because they bring the deep, low corner at the back out to you. Cupboards work well where the height is greatest, and open shelves or a bench seat suit a hallway.

Can you fit under-stairs storage to any staircase?

Yes. We measure your staircase at the free design visit and make every drawer and door to its angle, including staircases with winders, landings and open sides.

Can I still get to my meter or fuse board?

Yes. We plan access doors or a removable panel around meters, fuse boards, stopcocks and pipework so they stay easy to reach.

Will the drawers take heavy things like shoes and the vacuum?

Yes. We use heavy-duty soft-close runners rated for real loads, and we plan a space for the vacuum, its charger and anything else that lives under the stairs.

How much does bespoke under-stairs storage cost?

The price depends on the size of the staircase, how many pull-outs and drawers you choose, and the finish. After the free design visit you receive an itemised quote. Interest-free finance is available on projects over £6,000 with a 50% deposit, subject to status.

How long does it take to fit?

Most under-stairs storage is fitted in a day once it has been made in our workshop, which usually takes four to six weeks after you approve the design.

Which areas do you cover?

We are based in Peterborough and fit across an 80-mile radius, including Stamford, Oundle, Market Deeping, Bourne, Spalding, Huntingdon, St Ives, Ely, Wisbech, Oakham and Cambridge.
