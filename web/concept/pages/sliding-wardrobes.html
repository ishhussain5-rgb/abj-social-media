Are sliding wardrobes better than hinged?

Sliding wardrobes are better where space in front of the wardrobe is tight, such as beside a bed, because the doors need no room to open. Hinged doors give you access to the whole width at once. Your designer will show you both in 3D.

What finishes are available for sliding doors?

Mirror, tinted mirror, lacquered glass, woodgrain, painted and fabric-effect panels, in slim frames in black, bronze, silver or white. We bring samples to your home.

Can you replace just the sliding doors?

We specialise in complete made-to-measure wardrobes. If your existing wardrobe is sound, ask us at the design visit and we will tell you honestly whether new doors on your current carcasses would work.

Can sliding wardrobes go under a sloping ceiling?

Sliding doors work on full-height walls. Under a slope, angled hinged doors usually work best, and many loft rooms combine both.
