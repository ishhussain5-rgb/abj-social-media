Offer terms and conditions

Storage Upgrade (code STORE26)

Applies to fitted kitchen orders signed, with the 50% deposit paid, between 1 and 30 November 2026. Kitchens with a total order value of £8,000 or more (including VAT, after any discount) receive the Storage Pack: one pull-out recycling bin, cutlery and utensil inserts for two drawers and one 150mm pull-out spice or bottle rack. Kitchens of £12,000 or more also receive one pull-out larder or corner pull-out mechanism, chosen to suit the design. Items are supplied from ABJ’s standard storage range and fitted as part of the kitchen installation. They have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for a discount. Values quoted are ABJ’s standard prices for these items.

Christmas Glow (code GLOW26)

Applies to fitted bedroom and wardrobe orders of £3,000 or more (including VAT, after any discount) signed, with the 50% deposit paid, between 1 and 30 November 2026. Includes one LED strip and motion sensor per wardrobe run, from ABJ’s standard range, fitted as part of the installation. The LED package has no cash value. Orders signed and deposited by 20 November 2026 will be fitted before 24 December 2026, subject to the technical survey and to access to the property on the agreed dates.
