In detail

Home office and office fit-outs in Peterborough

ABJ Kitchens & Bedrooms designs, makes and fits bespoke home offices and workspaces across Peterborough, Stamford, Market Deeping, Huntingdon and the surrounding villages. We build fitted desks, shelving, filing and printer storage, and complete office refits for local businesses, including reception counters and meeting-room cabinetry.

Every piece is made to measure in our own workshop, so it can fit alcoves, loft rooms and awkward corners. We can match your office to the rest of your home, from storage to fitted bedrooms.

How much does a bespoke home office cost?

Cost depends on the room size, number of desks, materials, storage requirements and installation details. We provide a tailored quote after measuring your space and agreeing the design.

Do you design fitted desks and office storage?

Yes. We design fitted desks, wall storage, shelving, printer cupboards, filing storage and furniture for awkward spaces.

Can you help with cable management?

Yes. We plan access holes, cable routes and storage zones for chargers, monitors, routers and printers. Electrical work can be coordinated where required.

Do you renovate commercial offices?

Yes. We help with fitted furniture, storage, meeting-room cabinetry, reception counters and practical workspace refits for local businesses.

Can the office furniture match my existing interiors?

Yes. We can advise on finishes, colours, handles and details to coordinate with your existing kitchen, wardrobes, decor or commercial brand style.

How long does installation take?

Most fitted office installations take a few days, depending on size and complexity. Larger commercial refits are scheduled around the agreed scope of works.
