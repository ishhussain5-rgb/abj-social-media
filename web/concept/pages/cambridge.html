Which best describes your Cambridge home?

Cambridge has more of a mix than most cities. Pick the closest match and we will tell you what that usually means for your project.

Victorian & Edwardian terraces

These homes usually have a narrow, single-aspect kitchen at the rear, sometimes with a small extension. We focus on making every centimetre count, with slimline units, integrated appliances and full-height storage rather than fighting the room’s original footprint. Fitted wardrobes are also popular here for box rooms with no built-in storage.

See kitchen options

1930s & post-war semis

Often already extended, or a good candidate for a kitchen-diner knock-through. These projects usually involve a larger island or peninsula, more worktop run and a media wall in the adjoining living space, which we design as one project rather than three separate ones.

See media wall ideas

New-build estate homes

Most new-build kitchens arrive as a builder’s standard specification: perfectly usable, but rarely made to measure. The most common upgrade we are asked for is a full kitchen replacement plus fitted wardrobes throughout the bedrooms, both installed with minimal disruption because the property is otherwise finished.

See our wardrobes

Period & conservation-area homes

If your home is listed or sits in a conservation area, we design around those considerations and work sympathetically with features such as cornicing, fireplaces and original proportions. We always recommend checking any listed-building or conservation requirements with the council before work begins. Bespoke, in-keeping cabinetry suits these homes far better than standard modular units.

Whichever part of Cambridge you’re in, we’ve likely fitted a kitchen

The kind of homes we typically work on in each area, and what that usually means for your design.

South Cambridge

Trumpington & Trumpington Meadows

A mix of established family homes and the newer Trumpington Meadows estate close to the guided busway. New-build owners here often want to upgrade the standard-spec kitchen that came with the house, while nearby older properties tend to need kitchens re-planned around period proportions.

New-build upgradesHandleless kitchensFitted wardrobes
Central Cambridge

Newnham & Grantchester

Characterful Victorian and Edwardian homes, some in conservation areas along the Backs and towards Grantchester Meadows. We design kitchens and fitted storage that work with original proportions, cornicing and awkward chimney breasts rather than against them.

Period-sensitive designAlcove cabinetryShaker & in-frame
North Cambridge

Chesterton & Fen Ditton

Riverside terraces and post-war family homes close to the Cam. Many of these kitchens are narrower galley layouts, so clever storage and worktop planning matter more here than in wider open-plan homes elsewhere in the city.

Galley kitchen planningSmart storageUnder-stairs solutions
South-East Cambridge

Cherry Hinton

A well-established, family-heavy suburb with good-sized 1930s and post-war semis. Kitchen-diners and knocked-through extensions are common, which usually means larger islands, more worktop run and dedicated media wall space in the adjoining living area.

Kitchen-dinersIsland unitsMedia walls
West Cambridge

Cambourne

A larger new-build town roughly 20 minutes west of Cambridge. Homes here typically arrive with a builder’s-standard kitchen, so most enquiries are about replacing it entirely with something made to measure, plus fitted wardrobes for the bedrooms.

Builder-spec replacementFitted wardrobesHome offices
North Cambridge fringe

Histon & Impington

Popular commuter villages with a good mix of older cottages and modern infill housing. We regularly plan around sloping ceilings and box rooms here, ideal for made-to-measure wardrobes rather than off-the-shelf flat-pack furniture.

Do you cover homes in Cambridge conservation areas?

Yes. We regularly design bespoke cabinetry for period and conservation-area homes across Cambridge. We always recommend checking any listed-building or conservation-area requirements with the local council before work begins, and we’re happy to design around those constraints.

How far is Cambridge from your Peterborough workshop?

Around 45 minutes by road, comfortably within our roughly 80-mile working radius. Every kitchen, wardrobe and piece of furniture is made in our Peterborough workshop and brought to Cambridge for installation.

Do you work on new-build estates like Trumpington Meadows or Cambourne?

Yes, regularly. New-build kitchens usually arrive as a builder’s standard specification, so a common project here is a full made-to-measure replacement alongside fitted wardrobes throughout the bedrooms.

Is parking or access in central Cambridge a problem for the survey?

Not for the free design visit; we simply plan around it. For installation days in more restricted central areas, we’ll discuss parking and access with you in advance so the day runs smoothly.

Do you cover villages outside Cambridge itself?

Yes. Sawston, Fulbourn, Girton, Coton, Bar Hill, Willingham, Swavesey, Linton and the surrounding villages all fall within our usual coverage. If you’re unsure, just call and we’ll confirm.

What guarantee do you offer?

Every kitchen, wardrobe and piece of fitted furniture comes with a 10-year manufacturer guarantee, along with 0% finance options (minimum spend £6,000 with a 50% deposit), subject to status.
