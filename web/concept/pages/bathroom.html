In detail

Bespoke bathroom cabinetry in Peterborough

ABJ Kitchens & Bedrooms designs, makes and fits bespoke bathroom cabinetry for homes across Peterborough, Stamford, Market Deeping, Huntingdon and the surrounding villages. We build fitted vanity units, tall cabinets, cistern boxing and storage walls, with vanity tops in stone, quartz or laminate.

Every unit is made to measure in our Peterborough workshop from moisture-resistant materials, and designed around your existing pipework. We can coordinate plumbing and electrical work as part of the installation.

How much does bespoke bathroom cabinetry cost?

As a guide, individual vanity units and basin cabinets can start from around £900, while a complete fitted bathroom furniture suite with worktops and storage is typically higher. We provide a clear itemised quote after your free design survey.

Do you offer a free design visit for bathroom projects?

Yes. We visit your home, measure the space, discuss your requirements and prepare a design proposal. For many projects, this includes 3D visuals so you can see the layout before manufacturing begins.

Are your bathroom cabinets made from moisture-resistant materials?

Yes. We use moisture-resistant carcasses and waterproof-rated finishes throughout, designed to withstand the humidity and everyday wear of a working bathroom.

Can you fit cabinetry around existing plumbing and pipework?

Yes. We regularly design around existing pipework, boxed-in cisterns and awkward layouts, and can coordinate any plumbing or electrical changes as part of the installation.

Are your bathroom cabinets made to measure?

Yes. Every cabinet, vanity unit and worktop is manufactured to your exact measurements, which means we can work with alcoves, sloped ceilings and irregular room shapes.

Do you handle installation, plumbing and electrics?

Yes. We can manage the full installation, with plumbing and electrical work coordinated as part of your bathroom cabinetry project.

Do you cover my area?

We cover Peterborough and towns within roughly an 80-mile radius, including Stamford, Market Deeping, Spalding, Huntingdon, Wisbech, Oundle, Ely, Cambridge, Bedford, Northampton, Grantham and surrounding areas.
