Kitchens
What is the difference between in-frame, Shaker and handleless kitchens?

At ABJ, we offer four distinct kitchen styles to suit your home’s character. In-frame: the pinnacle of quality, where the door sits inside a visible solid wood frame, giving incredible durability and a classic British look. Shaker: a timeless favourite, with a flat centre panel and raised rectangular frame that balances modern simplicity with traditional warmth. Handleless: the minimalist’s choice, using J-pull or push-to-open technology for sleek, uninterrupted lines. Traditional: ornate details, raised panels and rich wood finishes for a more formal, grand room.

Do you provide quartz, granite and timber worktops?

Yes. We offer a full templating and installation service for luxury stone surfaces. Once your bespoke cabinets are installed by our team, our stone technicians visit to laser-template your surfaces. Your quartz or granite is then precision-cut in a factory environment and installed within 7–10 working days, including undermount sink cut-outs and drainer grooves.

Which appliance brands can I integrate into my design?

We work with all major appliance manufacturers, and supply and fit brands including Bosch, Neff, Siemens, AEG, Samsung and Rangemaster. We also fit Quooker boiling water taps and Elica extraction hobs, so your kitchen performs as well as it looks.

Wardrobes & bedrooms
Are your wardrobes truly bespoke or standard sizes?

Everything at ABJ is 100% bespoke. Unlike retailers who use filler panels to hide gaps around standard sizes, we make our cabinetry to your room’s exact measurements, including floor-to-ceiling heights and scribing around coving, skirting and angled chimney breasts.

What are my options for internal wardrobe storage?

Make the most of your space with options such as motion-sensor LED lighting, pull-down hanging rails, felt-lined jewellery drawers, integrated shoe racks and internal mirrors. Drawers run on premium soft-close hardware from GTV, Blum or Häfele, depending on the design.

Your project
How long is the typical lead time from design to installation?

Because we make our own cabinetry in our Peterborough workshop, lead times are often much shorter than national retailers. Typically, installation begins 4–6 weeks after the final design is signed off, and the installation itself takes 1–2 weeks depending on the plumbing and electrics.

Do I need to organise my own plumber or electrician?

No. We provide a complete service with one point of contact. Our team includes expert fitters, Gas Safe registered plumbers and NICEIC certified electricians, and we handle everything from removals and waste disposal to the final coat of paint.

Can you handle structural changes or plastering?

Yes. As part of our project management, we handle plastering, non-structural wall removal and flooring. For major structural changes that need lintels or building control, we work with trusted local contractors so your room is ready for your new furniture.

Materials & guarantee
What materials are your cabinets made from?

We use materials from trusted partners such as Egger, Kronospan and Cleaf. Our standard cabinets are built from 18mm high-density MFC with solid back panels for extra rigidity. For in-frame designs, we combine solid timber and moisture-resistant MDF for longevity in a kitchen.

What is the ABJ 10-year guarantee?

Every project we install comes with a 10-year guarantee on cabinetry and installation quality. Hardware and appliances are also covered by their manufacturers’ own warranties, which our team helps you register.
