In detail

Bespoke kitchens, wardrobes and bedrooms in Peterborough

ABJ Kitchens & Bedrooms designs, manufactures and installs bespoke furniture from our workshop at Brassey Close, Peterborough. We make fitted kitchens, fitted wardrobes, fitted bedrooms, media walls, storage and home offices, each one built to the exact measurements of your room.

Because we make everything ourselves, we can work with alcoves, sloping ceilings, uneven walls and period properties where standard units would leave gaps. Every project starts with a free design visit and a 3D design, and the furniture we make carries a 10-year manufacturer guarantee.

We cover Peterborough and towns within roughly 80 miles, including Stamford, Market Deeping, Spalding, Huntingdon, Ely and Cambridge.

How much does a bespoke fitted kitchen cost?

As a guide, straightforward fitted kitchens can start from around £5,000, while larger kitchens with premium worktops, appliances and installation work can be significantly higher. We provide a clear itemised quote after your free design survey.

Do you offer a free design visit?

Yes. We visit your home, measure the space, discuss your requirements and prepare a design proposal. For many projects, this includes 3D visuals so you can see the layout before manufacturing begins.

Do you cover my area?

We cover Peterborough and towns within roughly an 80-mile radius, including Stamford, Market Deeping, Spalding, Huntingdon, Wisbech, Oundle, Ely, Cambridge, Bedford, Northampton, Grantham and surrounding areas.

Are your kitchens, wardrobes and bedrooms made to measure?

Yes. We manufacture furniture around your measurements, which means we can work with alcoves, sloped ceilings, awkward corners, uneven walls and full wall-to-wall storage runs.

Do you manufacture in Peterborough?

Yes. ABJ designs and manufactures bespoke furniture from our Peterborough workshop, giving you more flexibility than standard-size showroom furniture.

Do you handle the full installation?

Yes. We can manage the design, manufacture and fitting process, with associated trades coordinated where required for kitchens and larger renovation projects.

Do you offer finance?

Finance options may be available subject to status through Ideal4Finance. We can explain the options available for your project after the design survey.

What guarantee do you offer?

We offer a 10-year manufacturer guarantee on the furniture we make, covering defects in materials and workmanship, with project details confirmed in your quote.
