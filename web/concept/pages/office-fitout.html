In detail

Office fit-out and refurbishment in Peterborough

ABJ Kitchens & Bedrooms designs, makes and fits office interiors for businesses across Peterborough, Cambridgeshire, Lincolnshire and Northamptonshire. We handle the joinery side of an office fit-out or refurbishment: reception desks, meeting room and boardroom furniture, staff kitchens, storage walls, display and wall panelling.

Everything is made to measure in our own Peterborough workshop, which means quicker fitting on site and finishes that match across the whole office. We work directly with you or alongside your builder, electrician and flooring contractor, and can fit outside your opening hours. For home working, see our home offices.

What does an office fit-out include?

We take care of the joinery: reception desks, meeting room and boardroom furniture, staff kitchens and tea points, storage walls, lockers, display units and wall panelling. We can work alongside your builder, electrician and flooring contractor for the rest.

Can you work outside our opening hours?

Yes. We can fit early, late or at weekends so your business keeps running, and because the joinery is made in our workshop, time on site is kept short.

Do you design the office?

Yes. We survey the space and show you the design in 3D before anything is made, so you can see the reception, meeting rooms and storage before you commit.

Do you fit out small offices?

Yes. We fit everything from a single reception desk or staff kitchen to a full floor.

Which areas do you cover?

We are based in Peterborough and work across an 80-mile radius, including Cambridge, Huntingdon, Stamford, Spalding, Wisbech, Ely and Northampton.
