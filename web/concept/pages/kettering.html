Planning a kitchen or bedroom in Kettering

Kettering’s Victorian and Edwardian terraces often have narrow kitchens, chimney alcoves and steep stairs. We plan for that from the start: made-to-measure units for the galley, alcove cupboards in the living room, and larger pieces built in sections so they fit up the stairs.

In Barton Seagrave and the new homes at Hanwood Park, most of our work is replacing developer kitchens, fitting wardrobes in empty bedrooms and building media walls in open-plan living rooms.

Do you cover Kettering, Barton Seagrave and Burton Latimer?

Yes. Kettering is about 30 miles from our workshop, around 45 minutes on the A14 and A605, and we fit across Barton Seagrave, Burton Latimer, Rothwell, Desborough and Geddington.

Can you make a narrow terrace kitchen work better?

Usually, yes. Made-to-measure units, full-height storage and a better layout make a real difference in a galley kitchen. If moving services would give you a much better kitchen, we’ll explain the trade-off at the survey.

Do you fit kitchens and wardrobes in new homes at Hanwood Park?

Yes. Replacing developer kitchens and fitting wardrobes and media walls in new homes is a big part of our work, and we plan around the existing plumbing and sockets where we can.

How much does a fitted kitchen cost?

A compact bespoke kitchen, made and fitted, starts from around £2,500, and a typical medium kitchen with quartz worktops comes to around £7,000 to £9,500. You get an itemised quote after the free design visit.

Can I see your kitchens and wardrobes in person?

Yes. Our showroom is at 3 Brassey Close, Peterborough PE1 2AZ, about 45 minutes from Kettering. Call 01733 686767 before you travel.
