Utility rooms and boot rooms in Peterborough

ABJ designs, makes and fits utility rooms, laundry rooms and boot rooms for homes across Peterborough and the surrounding towns. Whether you have a separate utility room, a space by the back door or a cupboard under the stairs, we make the cabinets, benches and tall storage to measure in our own workshop.

A utility room can match your kitchen, and a boot room can carry on into your hallway storage or under-stairs storage. Plumbing and electrics for appliances are arranged separately.

What is the difference between a utility room and a boot room?

A utility room is mainly for laundry and cleaning, with space for the washer, dryer and often a sink. A boot room is for coats, shoes and bags by the back or side door. Many homes combine the two.

Can you fit a utility area into a small space?

Yes. A stacked washer and dryer with a tall cupboard beside it fits into a surprisingly narrow space, and we can make cabinets as shallow or as tall as the room allows.

Can my utility room match my kitchen?

Yes. We can use the same doors, handles and worktops as your kitchen, whether we made it or not.

Do you plumb in the washing machine?

Plumbing and electrical connections are arranged separately. We plan the cabinets around where the water, waste and sockets are, and can recommend trades if you need them.

What does a utility room cost?

It depends on the size and how many cupboards and appliance spaces you need. As a guide, one recent set of fitted utility cabinets with lift-up doors came to around £2,100 made and fitted. You get an itemised quote after the free visit.

Which areas do you cover?

We are based in Peterborough and work across an 80-mile radius, including Stamford, Oundle, Market Deeping, Bourne, Spalding, Huntingdon, St Ives, Ely, Wisbech, Oakham and Cambridge.
