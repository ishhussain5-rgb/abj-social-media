The ABJ journey

Built on more than 20 years at the bench

ABJ was built in Peterborough on more than 20 years of hands-on joinery experience, with one clear goal: to challenge the flat-pack culture of the high-street kitchen market. Too many homeowners were forced to fit their design around standard cabinet sizes, or pay a premium for a “bespoke” label on rebranded standard units.

So we invested in our own workshop. By controlling the whole process, from the first 3D design to cutting the board and the final installation, we remove the delays, damage and miscommunication that hold the industry back. There are no commission-led sales agents here, just experienced designers and skilled makers.

Our Peterborough workshop

Where craftsmanship meets technology

Owning our manufacturing means we control quality at every step, which is why our furniture fits so precisely on installation day. Computer-controlled machines cut every board to a fraction of a millimetre, tough moisture-resistant edging protects every panel, and we build with boards from trusted names such as Egger and Kronospan, with premium soft-close hinges and runners.
