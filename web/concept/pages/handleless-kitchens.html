What is a handleless kitchen?

A kitchen with no handles on the doors. A recessed rail along the top of each door or drawer lets you open it with your fingertips, so the fronts stay flat and clean.

What is true handleless?

True handleless means the rail is built into the cabinet itself, rather than a lip shaped into the edge of each door. We make true handleless kitchens with rails in black, gold or nickel.

Are handleless kitchens practical?

Yes. The doors open easily from the rail, soft-close runners stop drawers slamming, and with no handles there is nothing to catch clothes on or knock into.

Matt or gloss?

Both work. Gloss reflects light and suits smaller or darker rooms, while matt hides fingerprints better. We bring samples of both to the design visit.

How much does a handleless kitchen cost?

A compact bespoke kitchen, made and fitted, starts from around £2,500, and a typical medium kitchen with quartz worktops comes to around £7,000 to £9,500. Your exact price comes with your 3D design.
