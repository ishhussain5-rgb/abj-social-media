Can you fit wardrobes under a sloping ceiling?

Yes. We measure the pitch of the ceiling at the free design visit and make angled carcasses and doors that follow your roofline, so the wardrobe runs wall to wall with no wasted gaps.

What can go in the low part under the eaves?

Where the ceiling is too low for hanging we use drawers, cupboards, shoe storage, open shelving or a dressing table. Long hanging goes where the ceiling is highest, and short hanging where it starts to drop.

Do you fit wardrobes in loft conversions and dormer rooms?

Yes. Loft conversions are some of the rooms we enjoy most. We design around dormer windows, skylights, steels, chimney breasts and loft hatches, and we can match the wardrobes to the rest of your bedroom.

Can I still get into the eaves space behind the wardrobe?

Yes. We can build access doors or removable panels into the design so you can still reach eaves storage, hatches, pipework or cables.

Can I have sliding doors under a sloping ceiling?

Sliding doors work on full-height walls. Under the slope itself, angled hinged doors usually work best, and your designer will show you both options in 3D.

How much do wardrobes for sloping ceilings cost?

The price depends on the length of the run, the number of angled sections, the doors and the interiors you choose. After the free design visit you receive an itemised quote, and interest-free finance is available on projects over £6,000 with a 50% deposit, subject to status.

We are based in Peterborough and fit across an 80-mile radius, including Stamford, Oundle, Market Deeping, Bourne, Spalding, Huntingdon, St Ives, Ely, Wisbech, Oakham and Cambridge.
