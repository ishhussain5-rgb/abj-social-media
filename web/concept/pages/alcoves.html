In detail

Alcove units and bookcases in Peterborough

ABJ Kitchens & Bedrooms designs, makes and fits made-to-measure alcove units for homes across Peterborough, Stamford, Oundle, Market Deeping, Huntingdon and the surrounding villages. Choose alcove cupboards with shelves above, full-height built-in bookcases, alcove desks, or alcove units as part of a media wall around the TV and fire.

Every unit is made in our own Peterborough workshop to the exact size of each alcove, then scribed to the wall so it looks built in. We can paint it to match your walls, or match it to your wall panelling.

How deep should alcove cupboards be?

Usually the depth of the chimney breast or a little less, so the units sit flush or slightly back. We measure at the free design visit and plan the depth around skirting, sockets and radiators.

Can you make both alcoves match if they are different sizes?

Yes. Each alcove is measured and made separately, and we design them so they look matched even when the spaces are not.

Can you build in lighting, sockets or the TV?

Yes. We can build in LED shelf lighting, space for AV equipment and cable routes, and design alcove units as part of a media wall.

Do you paint alcove units?

Yes. We supply them in a factory-quality painted finish in the colour of your choice, or in woodgrain and oak finishes.

How much do fitted alcove units cost?

The price depends on the size, the mix of cupboards and shelves, and the finish. After the free design visit you receive an itemised quote. Interest-free finance is available on projects over £6,000 with a 50% deposit, subject to status.

Which areas do you cover?

We are based in Peterborough and fit across an 80-mile radius, including Stamford, Oundle, Market Deeping, Bourne, Spalding, Huntingdon, St Ives, Ely, Wisbech, Oakham and Cambridge.
