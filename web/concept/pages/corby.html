Planning a kitchen or bedroom in Corby

A lot of Corby’s housing was built after it became a new town in 1950, so many homes have similar layouts: compact kitchens, box rooms and straightforward living rooms that suit a media wall. Made-to-measure furniture gets far more out of these rooms than standard sizes.

On the newer estates at Priors Hall Park, Little Stanion and Weldon, the starting point is usually a basic developer kitchen and bedrooms with no built-in storage. Those are the rooms where a better kitchen, fitted wardrobes or a media wall make the biggest difference.
