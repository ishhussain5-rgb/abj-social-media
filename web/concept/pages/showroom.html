In detail

Kitchen and bedroom showroom in Peterborough

ABJ Kitchens & Bedrooms has a showroom and workshop at 3 Brassey Close, Peterborough PE1 2AZ. Customers from Peterborough, Stamford, Huntingdon, Market Deeping and the surrounding area visit to compare kitchen doors, worktops, handles and wardrobe finishes, and to see the fittings that go inside every cabinet we make.

Because the furniture is made in our own workshop, the showroom is also where you can see how it is built. Before you visit, browse our project gallery and the fittings we use, or call 01733 686767.

Where is your showroom?

At 3 Brassey Close, Peterborough PE1 2AZ, next to our workshop.

Do I need an appointment?

You are welcome during our opening hours. It is worth calling 01733 686767 before you travel so a designer is free to show you round.

What can I see in the showroom?

Door styles and colours, worktop samples, handles, and the drawers, hinges and storage fittings we use, plus photos of rooms we have made for customers.

Can I get a quote at the showroom?

We can talk through ideas and rough costs there. For an exact price we measure your room at a free design visit and send a 3D design with an itemised quote.

Can you come to my home instead?

Yes. Most customers start with a free design visit at home, and visit the showroom to choose finishes.

Which areas do your customers come from?

We are based in Peterborough and work across an 80-mile radius, including Stamford, Oundle, Market Deeping, Bourne, Spalding, Huntingdon, St Ives, Ely, Wisbech, Oakham and Cambridge.
