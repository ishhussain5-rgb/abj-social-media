In detail

Kitchen fitters in Peterborough

ABJ Kitchens & Bedrooms designs, makes and fits kitchens from our workshop and showroom at 3 Brassey Close, Peterborough. Our fitters install the kitchens we build, which means the cabinets arrive made to your room and the team fitting them knows how they go together.

A standard installation covers the cabinets, doors, panels and the worktops on your quote. Plumbing, electrics, gas, removing the old kitchen, plastering and flooring can all be arranged and are listed on your quote, so you can compare it line by line with any other. Do you use your own kitchen fitters?

Yes. Your kitchen is fitted by ABJ's own team, the same business that designs it and makes it in our Peterborough workshop, so one company is responsible from the first measure to the handover.

How long does it take to fit a kitchen?

Usually one to two weeks, depending on the size of the room and how much plumbing and electrical work is involved. Your kitchen takes four to six weeks to make after you approve the design.

What does kitchen installation include?

Fitting the cabinets, doors, panels, plinths and the worktops listed on your quote. Plumbing, electrics, gas, removing the old kitchen, plastering and flooring can be arranged too, and are priced on your quote so you can see exactly what is included.

Do I need to find my own plumber or electrician?

No. We can arrange the plumbing, gas and electrical work as part of your project, so you have one point of contact. If you would rather use your own trades, tell us and we will work around them.

Can you fit a kitchen I bought elsewhere?

It is not something we usually do, because our fitters install the kitchens we make in our own workshop. If we do agree to fit a kitchen from another supplier, our guarantee does not apply to it.

When are stone worktops fitted?

Laminate and timber worktops are fitted with the cabinets. Quartz and granite are templated once the cabinets are in, then made and fitted within seven to ten working days.

Is the fitting guaranteed?

Yes. Your kitchen and its installation are covered by our minimum 12-month guarantee.

How much does a fitted kitchen cost in Peterborough?

A compact bespoke kitchen, made and fitted, starts from around £2,500, and a typical medium kitchen with quartz worktops comes to around £7,000 to £9,500. Your exact price, including fitting, comes with your 3D design.

Which areas do your kitchen fitters cover?

We are based in Peterborough and fit kitchens within about 80 miles, including Stamford, Oundle, Market Deeping, Bourne, Spalding, Huntingdon, St Ives, Ely, Wisbech, Oakham and Cambridge.
