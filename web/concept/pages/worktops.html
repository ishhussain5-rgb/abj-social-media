How much do quartz worktops cost in Peterborough?

The price depends on material, thickness, slab choice, kitchen size, cut-outs, joints, upstands and splashbacks. We recommend sending photos or booking a survey so we can give a realistic quote.

Are quartz worktops better than granite?

Quartz is usually easier to maintain and offers consistent colours. Granite is natural stone with unique patterns and character. The best option depends on your lifestyle and the look you want.

Can you replace worktops on an existing kitchen?

Yes. We can discuss replacement worktops for existing kitchens as well as new kitchen installations. We will check cabinet condition, levels, sink and hob positions before advising.

Do you supply upstands and splashbacks?

Yes. We can include matching upstands, full-height splashbacks and practical finishing details around sinks, hobs and walls as part of your quote.
