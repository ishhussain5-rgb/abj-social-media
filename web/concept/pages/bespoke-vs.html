When standard units are fine

An honest answer

If your kitchen is a simple square room with plenty of space, standard units can work well and may cost less. Bespoke makes the biggest difference in rooms with awkward walls, tall ceilings, alcoves or sloping ceilings, and when you want a finish or a size the catalogues do not offer.

In detail

Bespoke or standard kitchen: how to choose

When you compare kitchen quotes, look past the door style at what sits behind it: the thickness of the cabinet board, whether cabinets arrive assembled or flat-packed, the make of hinges and drawer runners, whether worktops and fitting are included, and who you deal with if something needs adjusting. A cheaper headline price can grow once fillers, end panels, delivery and fitting are added.

Is a bespoke kitchen more expensive than Howdens, Wren or Magnet?

Not always. Prices vary a lot between ranges and stores, so the fair test is to compare itemised quotes for the same room. Our compact kitchens start from around £2,500 made and fitted, and a typical medium kitchen with quartz worktops comes to around £7,000 to £9,500.

What is the difference between bespoke and made-to-measure?

They usually mean the same thing: cabinets built to your room’s sizes rather than chosen from a range of standard widths. Always ask whether the cabinets themselves are made to size, or just the doors and panels.

Are bespoke kitchens better quality?

Not automatically. Check the board thickness, whether the cabinets arrive assembled, the hinges and runners, and the guarantee. Ours are 18mm rigid cabinets with soft-close hinges and drawers, covered by a 12-month guarantee.

When is a standard kitchen the better choice?

In a simple square room with plenty of space and no awkward walls, standard units can work well and may cost less. Bespoke pays off in awkward rooms, tall rooms and when you want a size or finish that is not in a catalogue.

How long does a bespoke kitchen take compared with a standard one?

Ours take four to six weeks to make once you approve the design, then usually one to two weeks to fit. Standard ranges can be quicker if everything is in stock.
