In detail

Bespoke upholstery in Peterborough

ABJ Kitchens & Bedrooms makes bespoke upholstered headboards, beds, wall panels and window seats for homes across Peterborough, Stamford, Market Deeping, Huntingdon and the surrounding villages. Every piece is made to measure in a fabric you choose, and we also reupholster headboards, bed frames and bedroom chairs.

Upholstery can be designed alongside a fitted bedroom or wardrobes, so the headboard, bedside units and storage all work together.

How much does a bespoke upholstered piece cost?

Pricing varies depending on the type, size, and fabric chosen. Upholstered headboards start from approximately £350, and fully bespoke bed frames from £800. We always provide a detailed, transparent quote before any work begins so you know exactly what to expect.

Can you reupholster my existing furniture?

Absolutely. We offer a full reupholstery service for headboards, bed frames, bedroom chairs, and more. If your beloved furniture has seen better days, we can strip it back and rebuild it to the same high standard as a brand new piece, in a fabric entirely of your choosing.

What fabrics do you offer?

We offer an extensive range of premium fabrics sourced from the finest UK and European fabric houses. This includes velvets, bouclés, linens, performance fabrics, faux leathers, genuine leathers, and more, in a vast array of colours and textures. During your consultation we'll bring samples for you to see and feel in your own home.

How long does it take to make a bespoke piece?

Lead times typically range from 3–6 weeks from order confirmation, depending on complexity and our current schedule. Simpler pieces such as headboards are often quicker. We'll always confirm your expected delivery date clearly at the time of ordering.

Do you offer finance for upholstery projects?

Yes. We offer flexible finance plans, including 12 months interest-free credit (minimum spend £6,000 with a 50% deposit), to help you spread the cost of your bespoke upholstery. Our team can provide a personalised finance quote during your design consultation.
