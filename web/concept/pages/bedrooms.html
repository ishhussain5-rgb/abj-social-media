In detail

Fitted bedrooms in Peterborough

ABJ Kitchens & Bedrooms designs, makes and fits complete bedrooms for homes across Peterborough, Stamford, Spalding, Huntingdon and the surrounding villages. A fitted bedroom can include wardrobes, headboard walls, bedside units, dressing tables, desks and integrated lighting, all made to measure.

Because we manufacture everything in our own Peterborough workshop, we can work around loft slopes, chimney breasts, box sections and uneven walls. Our own team fits every bedroom, backed by a 10-year manufacturer guarantee.

What is included in a bespoke fitted bedroom?

A fitted bedroom can include made-to-measure wardrobes, bedside units, dressing tables, drawer packs, desks, shelves, headboards, beds, mirrors and integrated lighting. The exact combination is designed around your room and priorities.

Do you manufacture the furniture in Peterborough?

Yes. We survey and design the room, then manufacture the furniture in our own Peterborough workshop. This gives us greater flexibility over dimensions, colours, combinations and finishing details.

Can you work around sloped ceilings and awkward spaces?

Yes. Made-to-measure furniture is especially useful for alcoves, loft slopes, chimney breasts, box sections and uneven walls because each component is designed around the actual room rather than standard cabinet sizes.

Can you design children's and teenager bedrooms?

Yes. We can combine wardrobes, beds, desks, shelving and storage to create a practical room for sleeping, studying and organising belongings, with a design that can remain useful as the child grows.

How long does installation take?

Installation time depends on the size and complexity of the fitted package. Smaller projects may take only a few days, while full rooms with headboards, lighting and several furniture elements can take longer. The expected programme will be confirmed in your quotation.

Do you offer finance for fitted bedrooms?

Finance options may be available subject to status through Ideal4Finance. We can explain the available options once your bedroom design and quotation have been prepared.
