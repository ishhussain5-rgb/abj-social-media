What are bespoke fitted wardrobes?

Bespoke fitted wardrobes are made-to-measure storage solutions designed around your room, style and storage needs. They can be built wall to wall, floor to ceiling and around awkward features such as alcoves, chimney breasts or sloped ceilings.

Do you design and manufacture wardrobes in Peterborough?

Yes. We survey and design the project, then manufacture the furniture in our own Peterborough workshop. This gives us greater flexibility over sizes, colours, interiors and finishing details.

Can I customise the internal layout?

Absolutely. You can choose hanging rails, adjustable shelves, soft-close drawers, shoe storage, accessory trays, mirrors, lighting and other internal features to match how you use the bedroom.

Which wardrobe door types are available?

We offer hinged and sliding doors, including slab, Shaker, mirrored, glass and woodgrain options. Your designer will recommend a system based on the room, available clearance and preferred look.

How long does installation take?

Many fitted wardrobe installations are completed within a few days once the furniture has been manufactured. Larger walk-in rooms, detailed lighting or more complex fitted packages may take longer; the expected programme will be confirmed in your quotation.

Can you fit wardrobes under a sloping ceiling or in a loft conversion?

Yes. We measure the pitch with a laser at the free design visit and design angled carcasses and doors that follow your roofline, with hanging, shelves and drawers planned around the height available.
