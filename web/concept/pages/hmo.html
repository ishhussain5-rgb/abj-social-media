In detail

HMO renovations and fit-outs in Peterborough

ABJ Kitchens & Bedrooms fits out HMOs and shared houses for landlords across Peterborough and the surrounding area. We make and fit room furniture, wardrobes, desks, shared kitchens and storage, with durable finishes chosen for heavy daily use.

We work from architect drawings, room lists and agent briefs, and alongside your architect, agent or council. You look after the HMO licence and legal sign-offs; we design and fit the interior with HMO rules in mind.

KitchensWardrobesStorage
Do you sort the HMO licence?

No. You, the landlord, look after the licence and legal sign-offs. We design and fit the interior with HMO rules in mind, and we work alongside your architect, agent or council.

Can you fit out single HMO rooms?

Yes. We make and fit wardrobes, desks, storage, shelves, bedside units and space-saving layouts.

Do you also supply HMO kitchens?

Yes. We design and fit tough shared kitchens with handy storage, worktops, space for appliances and hard-wearing finishes.

What area do you cover?

We are based in Peterborough and cover 80 miles around it, including Cambridgeshire, Lincolnshire, Northamptonshire, Bedfordshire and nearby areas.

Can you work from drawings?

Yes. We can work from architect drawings, room lists, agent briefs or your own notes. We can also help shape the furniture plan.
