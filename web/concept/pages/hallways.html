In detail

Bespoke hallway storage in Peterborough

ABJ Kitchens & Bedrooms designs, makes and fits bespoke hallway storage for homes across Peterborough, Stamford, Market Deeping, Huntingdon and the surrounding villages. We build coat walls, shoe storage, benches, boot rooms and cloakrooms, and under-stair storage cut to the angle of your staircase.

Everything is made to measure in our own Peterborough workshop, so it can follow sloping stairs, skirting and uneven walls. We can add slatted or painted panelling to finish the space, and our own team fits every hallway.

How much does bespoke hallway storage cost?

Costs depend on size, internal storage, finishes, hardware and fitting requirements. Smaller hallway projects often start from around £1,500, while larger boot rooms and complex under-stair systems are quoted individually after the survey.

Can you work around angled stairs and uneven walls?

Yes. Made-to-measure manufacture allows us to follow sloping staircases, chimney breasts, alcoves, skirting, uneven walls and other details that standard furniture cannot accommodate properly.

Can you include seating, hooks and shoe storage?

Yes. We can combine upholstered or timber benches, coat hooks, hanging rails, shoe drawers, open cubbies, shelves and full-height cupboards in one coordinated design.

Do you provide 3D designs before manufacture?

Yes. We prepare clear visual designs so you can review the proportions, layout and finishes before approving the project for manufacture.

Do you manufacture the furniture yourselves?

Yes. ABJ manufactures made-to-measure furniture in our Peterborough workshop, which gives us control over dimensions, materials, quality and project coordination.

Can you provide supply only?

Yes. Supply-only manufacture is available for suitable projects, including trade customers and homeowners using their own competent installer. We will confirm the specification and collection arrangements in advance.
