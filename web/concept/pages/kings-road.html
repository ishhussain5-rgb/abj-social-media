The design

Built around how the family lives

The room is a large open-plan extension under a roof lantern, so the priority was to keep sightlines clear and the space feeling calm. A handleless light grey gloss kitchen does exactly that: no handles to catch the eye, and the gloss bounces the daylight around the room.

The island is the working heart of the kitchen. We set an induction hob into it with a pop-up downdraft extractor, so there is no overhead hood blocking the view across the room. A warm LED strip under the worktop edge gives the island a soft glow in the evening, and the far end becomes a breakfast bar with lit open shelving for books and everyday bits.

Along the back wall, floor-to-ceiling units house an American-style fridge freezer, built-in ovens and a coffee station framed in woodgrain with its own lighting.

Storage

A hidden arcade cupboard

What looks like another tall unit opens to reveal a retro arcade machine, built into the run so it disappears when the door is closed.

A bar wall for entertaining

In the dining area, dark woodgrain base units with lit glass display shelving turn the drinks collection into a feature.

A floating media unit

The living area has a wall-hung woodgrain media unit with open shelving, keeping the floor clear and the cables out of sight.

Storage that stays organised

Cutlery and utensil drawers are planned with dividers from day one, and tall housing keeps appliances tucked away.

Cost & programme

What does a project like this cost?

Every project is priced around the room, so here is how the investment and the timings are worked out.

Investment

Every ABJ kitchen is designed and priced around the room in front of us, so there is no off-the-shelf price for a project like this. The investment is shaped by the size of the space, the door and worktop finishes, the appliances you choose and how much tall, island and feature joinery is involved. Our bespoke kitchens typically start from around £5,000 for a compact room, and larger open-plan projects reflect their scale and specification.

After a free design visit you receive a clear, itemised quotation, so you can see exactly where the budget goes and adjust the specification before anything is made. 0% finance may be available, subject to status.

Typical programme

Once the design is signed off, the furniture is made to order in our Peterborough workshop, which typically takes around four to six weeks. Installation by our own fitters then usually takes one to two weeks for a kitchen, depending on the size of the project and worktop templating. Your quotation confirms the expected programme for your own home.
