Planning a kitchen or bedroom in St Neots

Many St Neots homes fall into two groups. The older terraces and cottages around the town centre and the river have alcoves, uneven walls and narrow staircases, so we build larger pieces in sections and scribe them to the walls on site.

The newer houses at Loves Farm, Wintringham and on the Eaton Socon estates usually come with a developer kitchen and empty bedrooms. Replacing the kitchen around the existing plumbing keeps costs down, and fitted wardrobes and a media wall are often added at the same time.

Do you cover St Neots, Eaton Socon and Eynesbury?

Yes. St Neots is about 30 miles from our workshop, around 40 minutes down the A1, and we fit across Eaton Socon, Eaton Ford, Eynesbury, Little Paxton and the villages nearby.

Can you replace the developer kitchen in a Loves Farm or Wintringham home?

Yes, it’s one of our most common jobs. Planning the new kitchen around the existing plumbing and sockets usually keeps the cost down, and we often add fitted wardrobes or a media wall at the same time.

Can you fit furniture in an older terrace with uneven walls?

Yes. We measure carefully, build larger runs in sections so they fit up narrow stairs, and scribe panels to the walls on site so there are no gaps.

How much does a fitted kitchen cost?

A compact bespoke kitchen, made and fitted, starts from around £2,500, and a typical medium kitchen with quartz worktops comes to around £7,000 to £9,500. You get an itemised quote after the free design visit.
