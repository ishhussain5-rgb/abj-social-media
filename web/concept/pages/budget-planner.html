In detail

Planning fitted furniture around a budget

Most people start with a figure in mind and no idea what it buys. The planner answers that the other way round: you give the figure, and it shows the size, style and finish that figure typically covers, using the same guide ranges as our cost guides.

Because everything is made to the room, the planner can only be a guide. Size, door style and worktop make the biggest difference, so two rooms on the same budget can look very different. Your designer will show you where the money goes and what each choice changes.

Is the budget planner a quote?

No. It is a guide based on recent quotes for work we have made and fitted. Your exact price comes with your 3D design after a free home visit.

Do I have to tell you my budget?

No, but it helps. When your designer knows the figure you are comfortable with, they can design to it from the start instead of showing you something you would not choose.

What does the budget cover?

Making your furniture to measure in our Peterborough workshop, delivering it and fitting it with our own team. Appliances, sinks and taps, plumbing, electrics and building work are priced separately; the planner lists what is separate for each choice.

What if my budget is lower than the planner shows?

Tell us anyway. We will say honestly what is possible, and where a different size, finish or worktop would bring the price down.

Can I spread the cost?

Interest-free finance is available on projects over £6,000 with a 50% deposit, subject to status.
