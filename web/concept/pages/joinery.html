In detail

Cabinet makers and bespoke joinery in Peterborough

ABJ Kitchens & Bedrooms is a Peterborough joinery and cabinet making company. We design, make and fit bespoke furniture for homes and businesses across Peterborough, Stamford, Oundle, Market Deeping, Huntingdon, Cambridge and the surrounding villages, from kitchens and wardrobes to media walls, alcove units, desks, display cabinets and bars.

If you are looking for a local carpenter, joiner or cabinet maker for fitted furniture, we are a full team with our own workshop: designers, cabinet makers and fitters. Every piece is made to measure, so it fits alcoves, chimney breasts, sloping ceilings and uneven walls exactly.

Are you a joiner or a kitchen company?

Both. We are a joinery and cabinet making company with our own Peterborough workshop, and kitchens, bedrooms and fitted furniture are what we make. That means one team designs, makes and fits everything.

Can you make a one-off piece of furniture?

Yes. As well as full rooms we make single pieces such as alcove units, desks, bookcases, display cabinets, bars and window seats.

Can you match furniture to what I already have?

Usually, yes. We can match colours, door styles and handles to existing furniture, and bring samples to compare at home.

Do you do general carpentry, like doors and skirting?

We focus on fitted furniture and joinery made in our workshop. If your project needs general building work, we can work alongside your builder.

Which areas do you cover?

We are based in Peterborough and fit across an 80-mile radius, including Stamford, Oundle, Market Deeping, Bourne, Spalding, Huntingdon, St Ives, Ely, Wisbech, Oakham and Cambridge.
