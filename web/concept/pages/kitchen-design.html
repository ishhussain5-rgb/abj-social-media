Is the kitchen design visit really free?

Yes. The design visit, the 3D design and the itemised quote are free, and there is no obligation to go ahead.

What happens at the design visit?

We measure the room, note where the plumbing, sockets, windows and extraction are, and talk through how you cook, eat and store things. Bring any photos or ideas you have saved.

What happens after the design visit?

Our designers draw your kitchen in 3D and price every part of it. We then go through it with you and change it until it is right.

Do I get an exact price before I commit?

Yes. The quote that comes with your design is itemised, so you can see the cost of every cabinet, worktop and accessory before you decide.

Can you design a kitchen for an extension or open-plan room?

Yes. We can work from your architect’s or builder’s drawings, plan the kitchen with the dining and living space around it, and visit again once the walls are up to measure precisely.

How much does a designed and fitted kitchen cost?

A compact bespoke kitchen, made and fitted, starts from around £2,500, and a typical medium kitchen with quartz worktops comes to around £7,000 to £9,500. Your exact price comes with your 3D design.

Which areas do you cover?

We are based in Peterborough and work across an 80-mile radius, including Stamford, Oundle, Market Deeping, Bourne, Spalding, Huntingdon, St Ives, Ely, Wisbech, Oakham and Cambridge.
