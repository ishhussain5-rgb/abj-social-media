Why GTV?

Big-name quality at a sensible price

GTV is a Polish furniture-fittings manufacturer supplying more than 50 countries. We started using their runners and drawer systems because they matched the big-name brands on quality at a sensible price, and we have never had a reason to change. Every part we sell is one we have fitted ourselves.
