In detail

Bespoke wall panelling in Peterborough

ABJ Kitchens & Bedrooms designs and fits bespoke wall panelling and feature walls for homes across Peterborough, Stamford, Market Deeping, Huntingdon and the surrounding villages. We make slatted timber walls, painted panelling and lit feature walls for living rooms, bedrooms, hallways and staircases.

Because we also make media walls, wardrobes and kitchens, we can match your panelling to the rest of your joinery. Every wall is made to measure in our Peterborough workshop and fitted by our own team.

How much does bespoke wall panelling cost?

Cost depends on the wall size, style, material, trims and finish. Simple panelling starts lower than detailed feature walls with lighting or storage. We provide a clear quote after measuring and discussing the design.

Can you design panelling for hallways and staircases?

Yes. Hallways and stair walls are ideal for bespoke panelling because it adds character and can help protect busy walls from everyday wear.

Do you paint the panelling after installation?

We can advise on paint-ready materials and finishing options. The exact painting arrangement can be discussed during your consultation depending on the project scope.

Can you include lighting in a feature wall?

Yes, where suitable. We can plan decorative joinery around LED lighting, media-wall details and electrical requirements. Any electrical work must be completed safely by a qualified person.

Can you match the decor to my wardrobes, kitchen or media wall?

Yes. Because ABJ designs fitted furniture and interiors, we can coordinate decorative wall details with wardrobes, kitchens, media walls, storage and other bespoke joinery.

How long does installation usually take?

Many straightforward panelling projects can be installed quickly, while larger feature walls or designs with lighting, storage or detailed trims take longer. We will confirm the expected timing with your quote.
