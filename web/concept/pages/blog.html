About the journal

Interior advice from a Peterborough joinery team

This journal is built around the questions we hear most during design visits: how to plan a kitchen island, how to choose practical wardrobe storage, how to hide media wall cables, and how to make awkward spaces work harder. Every article is written to support real renovation decisions, with clear guidance on layouts, finishes, storage, lighting, worktops and fitted furniture.

ABJ Kitchens & Bedrooms designs, makes and installs bespoke furniture from our Peterborough workshop for homes across Cambridgeshire and the surrounding counties.
