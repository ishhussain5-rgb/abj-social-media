In detail

Supply-only kitchens in Peterborough

ABJ supplies bespoke kitchens without installation to homeowners, builders, landlords and kitchen fitters across Peterborough and the surrounding area. The cabinets, doors and panels are the same ones we use in our fitted kitchens: made to measure from 18mm board, assembled in our workshop, with soft-close hinges and drawers.

A supply-only kitchen suits renovations where a builder is already on site, rental properties and anyone with a fitter they trust. If you would rather we fitted it, see our fitted kitchens, or start with our kitchen design service.

Do you sell kitchens without fitting?

Yes. We supply made-to-measure kitchens for your own fitter, builder or project, with the same design service and cabinets as our fitted kitchens.

Are supply-only cabinets flat-pack?

No. They are made from 18mm board and arrive assembled, ready to fit.

Is delivery included?

Delivery is quoted separately, so you only pay for what you need. Tell us the address and access when you ask for a quote.

Can you work from my builder’s drawings?

Yes. We can design from plans and measurements, and visit to check sizes once the room is built.

Can I get quartz worktops on a supply-only kitchen?

Yes. Quartz and stone worktops are templated and fitted on site once the cabinets are in, so we arrange that with you separately.

Do you supply kitchens to landlords and developers?

Yes. We supply and fit kitchens for rental properties and HMOs. Tell us how many kitchens you need and we will quote for them together.

Which areas do you cover?

We are based in Peterborough and work across an 80-mile radius, including Stamford, Oundle, Market Deeping, Bourne, Spalding, Huntingdon, St Ives, Ely, Wisbech, Oakham and Cambridge.
