In detail

Bespoke fitted kitchens in Peterborough

ABJ Kitchens & Bedrooms designs, makes and installs bespoke kitchens for homes across Peterborough, Stamford, Market Deeping, Huntingdon, Cambridge and the surrounding villages. Because every cabinet is made to order in our own workshop, we can work to non-standard sizes, awkward corners, sloping ceilings and older properties where off-the-shelf units would leave gaps.

Choose from handleless, Shaker and in-frame kitchens in any painted colour or wood finish, with quartz, granite, solid wood or laminate worktops, larder units, pull-out storage and integrated appliances. Our own fitters install every kitchen, and each one is backed by a 10-year manufacturer guarantee.

How much does a bespoke fitted kitchen cost?

Our bespoke kitchens typically start from £5,000, but the final investment depends on the size of the room, cabinet specification, style, materials, worktops, appliances and installation requirements. Your quotation is prepared after the design survey.

Do you manufacture your own kitchens?

Yes. Every cabinet is made to order in our Peterborough workshop. This allows us to create non-standard sizes, work around awkward spaces and maintain direct control over quality.

How long does design and installation take?

Lead times vary with the project and workload. As a broad guide, manufacture may take around four to six weeks after final design approval, while installation often takes one to two weeks depending on complexity and worktop templating. Your quotation will confirm the expected programme.

Which worktops can you supply?

We can supply and install quartz, granite, solid wood, compact laminate and high-pressure laminate worktops, together with matching upstands, splashbacks and full-height stone options where suitable.

Do you offer finance for new kitchens?

Finance options may be available subject to status through Ideal4Finance. We can explain the available options once your design and quotation have been prepared.
