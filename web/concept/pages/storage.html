In detail

Bespoke storage solutions in Peterborough

ABJ Kitchens & Bedrooms designs, makes and fits bespoke storage for homes across Peterborough, Stamford, Market Deeping, Huntingdon and the surrounding villages. We build under-stair storage, alcove units, larders, utility rooms, boot rooms and living room cabinets, all made to measure.

Because every piece is made in our own workshop, it can follow sloping stairs, uneven walls and awkward corners that standard units cannot. We can match colours and door styles to your fitted wardrobes or kitchen, and our own team fits everything.

How much does bespoke storage cost?

Cost depends on the size, materials, internal layout, hardware and installation requirements. We provide a clear quotation after measuring and discussing your design.

Can you build under-stair storage around awkward angles?

Yes. Under-stair spaces are rarely square, so we measure carefully and design drawers, doors and internal storage around the exact staircase angles.

Do you make alcove cabinets and shelving?

Yes. We design alcove units for living rooms, bedrooms, fireplaces and media areas, including cabinets, open shelves, hidden cable storage and matching finishes.

Can you design utility room or boot room storage?

Yes. We can plan tall cupboards, bench seating, shoe storage, coat hooks, appliance housings, laundry zones and practical cleaning storage.

Can the storage match my kitchen, wardrobes or existing furniture?

In many cases, yes. We can advise on colours, door styles, handles and finishes to complement your existing ABJ furniture, including our fitted wardrobes, or the rest of your home.

Do you provide 3D designs before manufacture?

Yes. For suitable projects, we provide clear design visuals so you can understand the layout, proportions and storage arrangement before manufacture begins.

How long does installation usually take?

Smaller storage projects may take a short installation, while larger utility rooms or multi-area projects can take longer. We will advise on timescale during quotation.
