H2: Light, Warm and Completely Handleless
P: The family wanted a kitchen that felt bright and luxurious but still worked hard every day. We designed a fully handleless scheme in a warm, light gloss, with slim gold-tone accents running through the fronts to pick up the brass of the pendant lights and stools.
P: The centrepiece is a long island finished with a waterfall marble-effect worktop that drops to the floor at both ends. It seats six, so it doubles as the family dining table, and the veining continues into a matching full-height splashback along the back wall.
P: Floor-to-ceiling tall units keep the ovens, fridge and everyday clutter behind flush doors, while concealed LED lighting under the wall units washes the splashback with light.
