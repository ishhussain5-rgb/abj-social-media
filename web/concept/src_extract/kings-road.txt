H2: Built Around How the Family Lives
P: The room is a large open-plan extension under a roof lantern, so the priority was to keep sightlines clear and the space feeling calm. A handleless light grey gloss kitchen does exactly that: no handles to catch the eye, and the gloss bounces the daylight around the room.
P: The island is the working heart of the kitchen. We set an induction hob into it with a pop-up downdraft extractor, so there is no overhead hood blocking the view across the room. A warm LED strip under the worktop edge gives the island a soft glow in the evening, and the far end becomes a breakfast bar with lit open shelving for books and everyday bits.
P: Along the back wall, floor-to-ceiling units house an American-style fridge freezer, built-in ovens and a coffee station framed in woodgrain with its own lighting.
