Kitchen prices in Peterborough: how to compare quotes

The fairest way to compare kitchen prices is to compare like for like. Ask each company for an itemised quote for the same room, then check the board thickness, whether the cabinets arrive assembled or flat-packed, the make of hinges and drawer runners, the worktop, and whether delivery and fitting are included. A low headline price can grow once fillers, end panels and fitting are added.

We can only give an exact price after a visit, because every kitchen is made to the room. Our surveyor measures up and gives you a rough idea of cost on the day, and the designer's quote that follows shows the actual price, including fitting. How much does a bespoke kitchen cost in Peterborough?

A compact bespoke kitchen, made and fitted, starts from around £2,500, and a typical medium kitchen with quartz worktops comes to around £7,000 to £9,500. Larger kitchens, islands, premium worktops and appliances cost more, and your itemised quote gives the exact price.

What is included in your kitchen prices?

The cabinets made to measure in our workshop, doors, handles, plinths and end panels, the worktops listed on your quote, and delivery and fitting by our own team. Appliances, plumbing and electrics, building work, plastering and flooring are extra unless they are on your quote.

Why can you not give an exact price before a visit?

Every kitchen is made to the room, so the price depends on the measurements and the choices you make. Our surveyor gives you a rough idea of cost at the free visit, and our designer's itemised quote gives the actual price, including fitting.

How much does a kitchen island add?

It depends on the size of the island, the cabinets inside it, the worktop and whether it has seating or a sink. We price it as part of your design, so you can see the difference it makes before you decide.

Is finance available on a new kitchen?

Yes. Interest-free finance for 12 months is available through Ideal4Finance on projects over £6,000 with a 50% deposit, subject to status.

Is a bespoke kitchen more expensive than Wren, Howdens or Magnet?

Not always. Prices vary a lot between ranges, so compare itemised quotes for the same room rather than headline prices. Our compact kitchens start from around £2,500 made and fitted.

How long does a bespoke kitchen take?

Four to six weeks to make once you approve the design, then usually one to two weeks to fit.
