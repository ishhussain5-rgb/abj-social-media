Aftercare and your 12-month guarantee

What our guarantee covers, how to tell us about a problem and what happens next, in plain English. The full wording is in our terms and conditions.

Contents

What our guarantee covers

For 12 months from the date of delivery, the furniture we make and all its component parts are guaranteed to be free from defects in design, workmanship, construction and materials. This covers manufacturing defects and workmanship.

If something has a valid defect, we will investigate and put it right at no cost to you. If a faulty item cannot be repaired properly, we will replace it at no cost.

Manufacturers’ warranties

Some materials and parts in your project, such as doors, cabinets, hinges, runners and fittings (GTV, Blum or Häfele, depending on the project), worktops and appliances, may also carry a warranty from the company that makes them. Whether one applies, how long it lasts and what it covers are set by that manufacturer, not by us.

Before you order, we will tell you which manufacturers’ warranties apply to your project and list them in your specification. If you need to claim, we will help you with it, although the manufacturer runs its own warranty.

Your legal rights

Our guarantee is in addition to your statutory rights, which it does not affect. Under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 you have up to 6 years to claim for defects that only show up later.

Kitchens bought elsewhere

We don’t usually fit kitchens bought from another company. If we do, our guarantee does not cover that kitchen.

How to report a problem

  1. Tell us within 30 days of spotting it. Call the office on 01733 686767 or email info@abjkitchensandbedrooms.co.uk and describe the problem. To claim under our 12-month guarantee, please put it in writing; an email is fine.
  2. Send photos if you can. A few photos on WhatsApp 07479 219421 help us understand the problem before anyone visits.
  3. We acknowledge it within 24 hours, to confirm we have your message and are looking into it.
  4. We look into it within a further 24 to 48 hours, depending on the problem. If a part made by another manufacturer is at fault, we deal with that manufacturer for you.
  5. We agree the fix with you. We explain what we found and the options to put it right, then agree a convenient date and time to come and do the work.

The 30-day notice applies only to our own 12-month guarantee, not to your statutory rights.

If you are still not happy

We will always try to reach a fair outcome with you. If you remain unhappy after our response, section 16 of our terms and conditions explains how to take your complaint further.
